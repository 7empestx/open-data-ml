## First project: windml

windml asks a simple question: can wind turbine faults be predicted from public 10-minute SCADA data? It uses every published year from two UK wind farms released by Cubico Sustainable Investments under CC BY 4.0.

<div class="odm-stats">
	<div class="odm-stat"><b>20</b><span>turbines at Kelmarsh and Penmanshiel</span></div>
	<div class="odm-stat"><b>9 years</b><span>of data, 2016 to 2024</span></div>
	<div class="odm-stat"><b>8.95 M</b><span>10-minute SCADA rows</span></div>
	<div class="odm-stat"><b>415</b><span>major faults labeled from the logs</span></div>
</div>

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="What we found" icon="magnifier">
		Under a strict test (settings picked on 2017 to 2020, scored once on 2021 to 2024), neither normal-behavior models nor short-term trip models beat chance at predicting gearbox oil-system or pitch faults. [Read the results](/windml/results/) and why.
	</Card>
	<Card title="What the data is still good for" icon="document">
		A look back at fleet health: where downtime came from, which turbines stand out, oil-pressure build-ups that ended at a service, and sensor or model drift. [See the sample report](/windml/condition-report/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Check our work" icon="github">
		The code is public under the MIT license, with tests on every push. `make setup && make smoke` reruns the pipeline on a slice of the data in about 10 minutes. [Reproduce it](/windml/reproduce/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="How we work with data" icon="open-book">
		Open licenses only, credit to the people who published the data, and no resale. Operator data stays the operator's. [Read the data policy](/about/).
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## For wind operators

If you run wind turbines, we can produce the same kind of condition report from your own SCADA exports and status logs. The pilot is free. You send a year or more of data, and two weeks later you get a written report with a per-turbine scorecard and specific things to check. You keep ownership of your data.
