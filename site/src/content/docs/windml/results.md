**No model results are published yet.**

The data has been cleaned and fault-labeled. Models haven't been trained and tested in public yet.

When results are published, each one will include:

- which data and which years were used,
- how the data was split for training and testing,
- the metrics used, such as how many days before a labeled fault a flag appeared and how many false alarms there were per turbine per month,
- results with and without the 19 July 2022 heatwave stops, and
- the limits of what the result shows.

Results based on public data only describe these wind farms and these years. They aren't a guarantee about any other turbine or site.
