## First project: windml

windml looks for early signs of wind turbine faults in 10-minute SCADA data.

It starts with two UK wind farms released by Cubico Sustainable Investments under CC BY 4.0:

- **Kelmarsh:** 6 turbines, all of 2022, 315,360 rows of 10-minute data.
- **Penmanshiel:** 5 turbines, 130,320 rows of 10-minute data.

So far the data has been cleaned and checked, and major faults have been labeled from the farms' own event logs: 12 at Kelmarsh and 2 at Penmanshiel.

There are no model results yet. We'll post them once they've been tested.

## What's next

- Add more years of Kelmarsh and Penmanshiel data.
- Build models that learn each turbine's normal behavior and flag when it drifts.
- Test whether those flags show up in the days and weeks before the labeled faults.
- Publish the results, including what doesn't work.
- Add more open datasets as new projects.
