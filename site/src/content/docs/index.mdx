## First project: windml

windml tests whether wind turbine faults can be predicted from public 10-minute SCADA data.

It uses two UK wind farms released by Cubico Sustainable Investments under CC BY 4.0:

- **Kelmarsh:** 6 turbines, 2016 to 2024, 2.84 million rows of 10-minute data, 97 major faults labeled from the farm's own logs.
- **Penmanshiel:** 14 turbines, 2016 to 2024, 6.11 million rows, 318 major faults labeled.

**The first result is negative.** Under a strict walk-forward test, with settings picked on 2017 to 2020 and scored once on 2021 to 2024, neither normal-behavior models nor short-term trip models beat chance at predicting gearbox oil-system or pitch faults. [Read the results](/windml/results/), including why.

The same data still supports a useful look back. The [sample condition report](/windml/condition-report/) shows where downtime came from, which turbines stand out, and which abnormal states lasted for months.
